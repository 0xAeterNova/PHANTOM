# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a harassment-free, accessible, and respectful research community. This project concerns intimate signals—voices, faces, language, safety concerns, age, and presentation—so contributors must respect consent, privacy, dignity, cultural difference, disability, and the right to be uncertain or unobserved.

## Expected behavior

- Use inclusive, non-diagnostic, non-stereotyping language.
- Treat model outputs as fallible observations, never facts about a person.
- Obtain permission before sharing any human data; prefer synthetic fixtures.
- Accept corrections, disclose uncertainty, and document negative results.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect names, pronouns, accessibility needs, and boundaries.
- Report security, privacy, and safety problems privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, or discriminatory remarks.
- Uploading recordings, faces, transcripts, personal data, credentials, restricted datasets, or unlicensed weights.
- Building covert surveillance, identity recognition, deception detection, diagnostic, punitive, or high-impact decision features.
- Claiming that voice, face, age, or presentation proves emotion, identity, health, honesty, or intent.
- Evading dataset terms, authentication, consent, safety routes, or deletion controls.
- Pressuring a user to depend on the assistant or discouraging human support.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository spaces, reviews, demonstrations, studies, and public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject changes, restrict participation, or notify an appropriate platform when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer channel designated by the repository owner. Do not include personal or crisis-related details in a public issue. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, avoid conflicts of interest, minimize copied sensitive information, and communicate an outcome when safe.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant while adding project-specific responsible-AI and data-governance rules. If an official contact is not yet configured, contact the repository owner privately rather than opening a public report.
